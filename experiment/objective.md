After performing this experiment, the learner will be able to:

- Understand the concept of creep and time-dependent deformation in engineering materials.
- Study the creep behaviour of lead under a constant applied load.
- Observe how deformation increases with time even when the applied load remains constant.
- Measure the extension of the specimen at regular time intervals during the test.
- Plot the variation of extension with time and identify the creep behaviour.
- Understand the influence of temperature and material properties on creep.
- Recognize the importance of creep analysis in the design of structures and machine components subjected to sustained loading.
